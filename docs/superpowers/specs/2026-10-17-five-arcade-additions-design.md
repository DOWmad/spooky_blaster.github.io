# Five Arcade Additions

The six-level campaign keeps its current controls, scoring flow, and two visual themes. Five additions create new positioning and timing decisions.

1. **Banshee / Siren Drone:** One appears in levels 4–6. It pauses near the top, marks a safe gap for 0.75 seconds, then sends a descending wall. Drones cannot block the wall; the player can dodge through its gap. The wall disappears below the screen, and the enemy leaves after firing.
2. **Hex Bolt / Chain Lightning:** A secondary pickup occupies the existing weapon slot. A forward shot deals 1.5/2/2.5 damage by level, then jumps to two/three/four nearby enemies for 80% damage at each target. It never repeats a target in one chain, and it is weaker against a lone boss than rockets or the laser.
3. **Witching Hour / Chrono Cell:** A rare pickup slows enemy projectiles to 30% speed for three seconds. Enemies, the player, and the boss continue at normal speed. Pause stops the timer. A black-cat freeze still overrides projectile movement.
4. **Mirror Witch / Mirror Core:** From the second campaign loop, this replaces the level-two boss. Only the lit side takes aimed damage; its opening changes side after every attack. Bombs hit through the mirror. The boss gets a 70-second berserk clock so the positioning challenge has room to play out.
5. **Graze Capacitor:** A pickup grants a ship upgrade until the next death. Each hostile bullet awards 25 points once when it passes close to an exposed ship without hitting. At max main-gun power it also adds one ultimate charge. Invulnerability and an active ultimate suppress graze rewards.

All additions use the existing pixel-art and sound systems. The title card explains the pickups, and the Mirror boss HUD labels its vulnerability. The two themes share mechanics with different names and colors.
